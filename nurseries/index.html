Analysis & Recommendations May 2026

The picture overall

Seven of the eight active settings hold a current Good rating. None are Outstanding — except the Ducklings chain, whose new location is as yet uninspected. The two most recent inspections are Acorn Shenley (Feb 2025) and Wellies (Aug 2024), both reconfirming Good from previous Good ratings — the most reassuring pattern an Ofsted history can show.

Availability clusters around September 2027 for most waitlisted options, with Ducklings (Sep 2026) as the only potential earlier entry point.

Rule out with confidence

Ashbourne at Oxley Park is the most concerning setting in the cohort. A welfare requirements notice in June 2025 — a legal instrument, not a routine advisory — covering safeguarding, first aid, and staff:child ratios, followed by a second ratio breach just five months later, is a pattern rather than an isolated slip. The Good rating from 2022 predates all of this. The visit impression lines up with the paperwork.

Webber had a 2023 safeguarding allegation notice on top of a first inspection with notable improvement points around routines and letter-sound accuracy. Combined with the visit impression, an easy pass.

Best positioned

Woodlands Clean record, formerly Outstanding, already registered on waitlist. The standout detail is child-led routines for babies — sleep and eat on demand — which is genuinely rare at this scale. Forest school adds further weight.
Wellies Largest setting (125 places), most recently reconfirmed Good (Aug 2024), no compliance history. An on-site farm and a strong culture around books and maths make it distinctive. The only improvement note — pace interactions for younger children — is minor and very common.
Acorn Shenley Freshest inspection of the entire cohort (Feb 2025). The settling-in programme — including home visits before a child starts — is the most thoughtful approach to transitions across all eight settings. The only improvement point (maths planning) is the lightest criticism in the whole set. Worth prioritising.
Ducklings Unknown quantity for this specific location, but an Outstanding-rated chain running 9+ MK settings for over 20 years. If the standards carry through, this could be the best option on the list. Worth visiting early once the opening is confirmed.
Acorn Cold Harbour Good first inspection in 2022, Forest School grounds, strong SEND support. The 2021 supervision action predates the inspection and appears resolved. Unvisited — worth seeing before committing given September 2027 availability.
Acorn at the Rowans Positive trajectory — moved from Requires Improvement to Good in 2023. Visited and availability confirmed, registration pending. The RI history is worth bearing in mind against the other Acorn settings (Shenley, Cold Harbour), which have cleaner records.
